# 1. Who is this? Look up one person
$ ldapsearch -x -LLL -H ldap://ldap.forumsys.com -b "dc=example,dc=com" "(uid=euler)" cn objectClass
   dn: uid=euler,dc=example,dc=com
   objectClass: inetOrgPerson
   objectClass: organizationalPerson
   objectClass: person
   objectClass: top
   cn: Leonhard Euler

# 2. Who is in this group?
$ ldapsearch -x -LLL -H ldap://ldap.forumsys.com -b "dc=example,dc=com" "(ou=mathematicians)" uniqueMember
   dn: ou=mathematicians,dc=example,dc=com
   uniqueMember: uid=euclid,dc=example,dc=com
   uniqueMember: uid=riemann,dc=example,dc=com
   uniqueMember: uid=euler,dc=example,dc=com
   uniqueMember: uid=gauss,dc=example,dc=com
   uniqueMember: uid=test,dc=example,dc=com

# 3. The question a system asks: is this person in the group?
$ ldapsearch -x -LLL -H ldap://ldap.forumsys.com -b "dc=example,dc=com" "(&(ou=mathematicians)(uniqueMember=uid=euler,dc=example,dc=com))" dn
   dn: ou=mathematicians,dc=example,dc=com
$ ldapsearch -x -LLL -H ldap://ldap.forumsys.com -b "dc=example,dc=com" "(&(ou=mathematicians)(uniqueMember=uid=newton,dc=example,dc=com))" dn
   (no match)
   -> euler: yes, newton: no. One list answers every system the same way.
