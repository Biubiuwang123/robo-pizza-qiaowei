The story

Maple Hill Elementary is throwing a class party, and the teacher sends Robo, the school's helper robot, to order pizza. Simple errand. Except the teacher has to sign in, the party site has to be the real one, Robo needs a badge the kitchen will accept, the pizza shop's password has to stay secret, and every staff member needs exactly the right access.

Each of those is a real problem, and each one is a product someone had to build.
