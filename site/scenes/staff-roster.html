The staff roster

Scene 1 was the party site's sign-in for visitors. This one is for the people who work at Maple Hill, and the six school systems they use every day.

1 A new teacher starts

Mr. Okafor joins on Monday. He needs the five everyday systems.

🤖 Without a roster, every system keeps its own list. Add him to each one.

2 Groups, not people

Systems don't list people. They list groups, then ask the roster who's in them. Mr. Okafor also volunteers for the party committee.

The pizza account asks the roster: is okafor in "Party committee"? no

That question travels in LDAP, the language directories speak.

3 Someone leaves

Ms. Chen moves to another school in June. Her access has to go, everywhere.

4 Sign in once for the day

Ms. Rivera signs in at her classroom computer in the morning. The roster checks her password once and hands her a ticket good until evening.

Day ticketMs. Rivera · Maple Hill · good until 6 pmKerberos ticket

5 The roster can't go down

If the roster is unreachable, nobody can sign in to anything. The roster lives in the main building.

The copies are domain controllers; a managed directory keeps them in separate Availability Zones.

Who builds staff rosters

  • Run your own: Microsoft Active Directory on your own servers, or open-source OpenLDAP and FreeIPA.
  • Active Directory, managed in a cloud: AWS Directory Service, Microsoft Entra Domain Services, Google Cloud Managed Microsoft AD.
  • Directories born in the cloud: Microsoft Entra ID, Okta Universal Directory, JumpCloud.
  • Already have a roster? Connect to it instead of copying it: AWS's AD Connector forwards questions to a directory you already run.
Peek at a real directory

Real LDAP, the same questions a school system would ask, sent to Forum Systems' public read-only test directory (its sample people are famous mathematicians and scientists). Anonymous searches only; nothing is changed.

Run it yourself: lab/staff_roster.py. A managed Active Directory answers the same questions, inside your private network.

The smallest staff roster that works

Ticks as you play

  1. Keeps one list every system checks
  2. Grants access by group, not person by person
  3. Answers "is this person in that group?" for any system
  4. Removes someone everywhere at once
  5. Checks the password once, then hands out a day ticket
  6. Keeps a second copy, so sign-in never goes down

That's the whole product. Everything else is scale and polish.
