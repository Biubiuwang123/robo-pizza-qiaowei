Part two

Robo's own badge

The website badge proves the party site to the whole world. The kitchen is different: it's the school's own system, and it only lets in robots the school itself vouches for. So the school runs its own badge office.

5 Set up the school's badge office

Build it in three moves.

  1. Master stampLocked in the vault. Only ever used to make desk stamps.root CA · offline
  2. Desk stampSigned by the master stamp. Does the daily stamping, so the master can stay locked away.subordinate CA
  3. Robo's badgerobo.school.example · good for 1 dayleaf certificate

6 Knock on the kitchen door

At this door, both sides show a badge.

Visitor → kitchen
Kitchen → visitor

Both sides checking each other is mutual TLS. The kitchen's short list of stamps it accepts is its trust store.

7 Robo's key gets copied

At the repair shop, someone copies Robo's memory, secret key included. Now a thief can show Robo's badge and pass the challenge.

The copy works for at most 1 day, even if nobody notices.

Cancelled badgesrobo.school.example · serial 1000 · key copied

The cancelled list is a CRL. A live "is this badge still good?" check is OCSP.

Who runs badge offices

  • Public stamp offices that every browser trusts, such as Let's Encrypt.
  • Badge office as a service: request, prove, and re-stamp handled for you. On AWS that's AWS Certificate Manager. Cloudflare, Google Cloud and Azure offer their own.
  • A private badge office for badges only your own systems trust. Run it yourself with tools like step-ca or HashiCorp Vault, or use a managed one such as AWS Private CA or Google Cloud's Certificate Authority Service. The managed kind runs the stamps and publishes the cancelled list for you.
Peek at a real private badge office

Part two, for real: a master stamp, a desk stamp, and badges built with openssl on a laptop, then real handshakes between Robo and the kitchen.

# Building the school's badge office (openssl, EC P-256)
master.crt   subject=CN=Maple Hill Master Stamp
             issuer=CN=Maple Hill Master Stamp
desk.crt     subject=CN=Maple Hill Desk Stamp
             issuer=CN=Maple Hill Master Stamp
robo.crt     subject=CN=robo.school.example
             issuer=CN=Maple Hill Desk Stamp
kitchen.crt  subject=CN=kitchen.school.example
             issuer=CN=Maple Hill Desk Stamp

# 1. Robo knocks on the kitchen door (mutual TLS)
   kitchen sees robo.school.example, TLSv1.2, ECDHE-ECDSA-CHACHA20-POLY1305
   robo sees kitchen.school.example, reply: Come in.
   -> ACCEPTED

# 2. An outside drone with a badge from another office knocks
   kitchen's check: unable to get local issuer certificate
   -> REJECTED

# 3. Robo's key was copied: cancel its badge, publish the cancelled list
   CRL: Serial Number: 1000
   CRL: Key Compromise
   kitchen's check: certificate revoked
   -> REJECTED

Run it yourself: lab/badge_office.py. This is the do-it-yourself version; a managed private CA does the stamping and the cancelled list for you.
