3 Earn a badge

Now you run the real party site. Anyone can ask Trusty Stamps for a badge that says party.maplehill.example, so how does it know you're the owner?

party.maplehill.example's front door Note: 7f3a…c91e

"Pin this note on your front door. Only the real owner can."

The "front door" is the domain's public notice board (DNS). The note is a CNAME record.

Who runs badge offices

  • Public stamp offices that every browser trusts, such as Let's Encrypt.
  • Badge office as a service: request, prove, and re-stamp handled for you. On AWS that's AWS Certificate Manager. Cloudflare, Google Cloud and Azure offer their own.
  • A private stamp office for badges only your own systems trust, like the one Robo will carry to the kitchen. That's the next act, with AWS Private CA.

The smallest badge office that works

Ticks as you play

  1. Says who the badge belongs to
  2. Says who vouches for it
  3. Has a seal that can't be forged
  4. Proves the holder has the secret key
  5. Checks the requester owns the name
  6. Expires on purpose
  7. Re-stamps before expiry, without a human

That's the whole product. Everything else is scale and polish.
